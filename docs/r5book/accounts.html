Accounts and sessions

More than one person

R5 has accounts: one administrator and, if they want, standard accounts for other people. Someone signs in, works, locks the screen or logs out. A program does not draw those screens, but it runs inside that session, and a few things follow from that.

Who your program runs as

  • Your program runs as the person who signed in, never as root. The administrator, login name user, has a uid of 1000 or higher like everyone else.
  • Only the system services stay at uid 0: the kernel, launch_daemon, the registrar, the network and package services, and Login itself.
  • More than one person can be signed in at the same time (fast user switching, build 10074). Each signed-in person has their own app_server desktop, workspaces, clipboard, key store, notification server, Deskbar and Tracker. While another person's desktop is shown, your apps keep running, your sound is muted and your notifications wait until you return.

Folders and settings

Each person has their own home, Desktop and config/settings. Ask the system where they are; never write /boot/home into your program.

Do

find_directory(B_USER_SETTINGS_DIRECTORY, &path) for your settings, and getpwuid(getuid()) when you need the person's name or home.

Don't

Hardcode /boot/home/config/settings. For a standard account that is someone else's folder, and your program cannot write there.

BPath path;
if (find_directory(B_USER_SETTINGS_DIRECTORY, &path) == B_OK) {
	path.Append("MyApp settings");
	// /boot/home/config/settings/... for the administrator,
	// /boot/home/users/nicky/config/settings/... for Nicky
}

A setting that is for the whole computer lives in B_SYSTEM_SETTINGS_DIRECTORY, and only an administrator changes it.

Changes that need an administrator

Some changes are for the whole computer: the screen mode, the network, the clock, accounts. Do not run your program as root for them. In R5's own windows such a page is gray until the person opens the lock with an administrator password. Accounts are then changed by a small checked helper, not by the window running as root. One unlock covers every such page, and the grant ends at the lock screen and at log out.

No public call yet. The lock and the helper are used by Settings and Users & Accounts. A public call for other programs is not part of LibR5 yet. Until then, keep system-wide changes in those windows.

Lock, switch user and log out

Deskbar asks Login, through r5_admin_send(), to lock the screen, switch user, or log out. The kernel names the sender, so a person can only act on their own session.

MessageTo (admin port)What happens
'lokc'application/x-vnd.Haiku-LoginSwitches to Login's desktop and shows the lock screen. Programs keep running, sound is muted, notifications wait.
'swtu'application/x-vnd.Haiku-LoginLocks the current session and shows Sign in with a green dot on signed-in accounts.
'lout'application/x-vnd.Haiku-LoginEnds only this person's session and destroys their desktop. Other signed-in people keep running.
BMessenger login("application/x-vnd.Haiku-Login");
if (login.IsValid()) {
	BMessage req('lokc'), reply;
	r5_admin_send(login, &req, &reply);	// lock the screen
}

When the person logs out

Every program of that person's uid gets B_QUIT_REQUESTED. Save what you need in QuitRequested() and quit. Do not open a dialog that waits for an answer: the person has already chosen to leave, and Login does not wait for it. Other signed-in people are unaffected.

Do

Save quietly, for example a draft next to the document, and return true.

Don't

Ask "Save changes?" and return false until someone answers. Nobody will.

Try R5 and the first password

The R5 image is the live medium: it holds /boot/system/settings/r5-live-medium, and the Installer leaves that file out.

Try R5An installed R5
PasswordNone. Sign in says "You're trying R5. Passwords for mail and online accounts are forgotten when you shut down."An account without one chooses it on Sign in, once, before its first desktop: name, password twice, the strength hint.
BKeyStoreWorks, in memory only. Gone at log out or shut down.Encrypted on disk, unlocked by the password at Sign in.

For your program nothing changes: store secrets with BKeyStore as always. In Try R5 they last for the session, so ask again next time instead of treating a missing key as an error. Passwords are stored as Argon2id (crypt()).

The guest and Shared

  • The guest is off by default. An administrator turns it on. At log out everything the guest made in its home is deleted.
  • Recognise the guest with r5_admin_is_system_guest(getuid()), never by the name: an older account may be called "guest" too. The guest has no lock screen.
  • /boot/home/Shared is for everyone (mode 01777). Anyone can put a file there; only its owner, the administrator whose desktop is currently shown, and the system can delete it.
  • The protection holds for Shared itself, as with /tmp. A folder made inside Shared belongs to its maker, who can delete what others put in it.

Do

Offer Shared, or a removable disk, when a guest saves something to keep.

Don't

Keep a guest's work in its home after log out. It is deleted.
