The Appearance Kit

The parts R5's own windows are built from: Settings, About, Display, Sign in, and the lock screen. All classes live in namespace BAppearanceKit, follow the system colors and font size, and can be archived.

What is in this kit

  • Cards & layout — BActionCard, BCard, BCardStrip, BCardStripItem.
  • Controls & views — BAccentButton, BAxisBar, BIconView, BPasswordControl, BScreenArrangement, BTiltLevel.
  • Settings & accounts — BAdminLockBar, BPageHeader, BSidebar, BSidebarItem, BUserCard, BUserListItem.
  • Functions & effects — r5_screen_draw(), r5_avatar_draw(), and window glass/shadows.
  • Theming — themes, dark mode, the R5 Modern controls, and how a program follows them.
